ReThinky Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

ReThinky is made by 24bit7, the developer name of Ben Walker-Williams, an individual developer in the United Kingdom. If you have a question about this policy or your data, email bensbookshelves@gmail.com.

The Short Version

  • There's no ReThinky account, no adverts and no tracking.
  • Your quizzes, scores and documents stay on your phone.
  • When you ask ReThinky to make or check a quiz, the text it needs goes straight from your phone to the AI service whose key you added. It goes nowhere else.
  • 24bit7 has no server. We never see your quizzes, your documents or your keys.

What Stays on Your Phone

  • Your quizzes, answers, scores and when each question is next due.
  • Anything you add or share to ReThinky: documents, web links, pasted text and anything in the Stash.
  • Interview Prep: your CV, the job advert, your answers and notes.
  • Your settings.
  • Your AI keys. These are encrypted with a key held in your phone's secure hardware (the Android Keystore), and are only ever sent to the AI service they belong to.

What Leaves Your Phone, and Where It Goes

ReThinky only sends something when you ask it to do a job that needs it.

To your AI service. Making a quiz, rewriting or checking questions, rating or suggesting an answer, tagging the Stash, learning new fun facts and Interview Prep all use an AI. ReThinky sends the text that job needs (for example the document you picked, or your CV and the job advert for Interview Prep) with your key, directly to the AI service you chose:

  • Google (Gemini): policies.google.com/privacy
  • Anthropic (Claude): anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • OpenAI: openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • Mistral AI: mistral.ai/terms
  • OpenRouter: openrouter.ai/privacy
  • DeepSeek: deepseek.com/privacy

What happens to it there is covered by that company's own policy. Worth knowing:

  • On Google's free Gemini tier, Google may use what you send to improve its products. The paid tier doesn't do this. Interview Prep never runs on Gemini's free tier, so a CV added there never goes to it.
  • Anything you share into the Stash is read by your AI so it can be given a tag, such as CV or Article.
  • DeepSeek is based in China, and what you send is stored there.

For Interview Prep, ReThinky also asks the AI to search the web about the company and the role.

To YouTube, through Gemini. When you make a quiz from a YouTube video, the link is sent to Gemini, which watches the video.

To Wikipedia. When you make a quiz from a topic, your search words are sent to Wikipedia to find the article.

To the web page you chose. When you add a link, ReThinky fetches that page directly, the same way a browser would.

Backups

  • Backup in Settings saves a file wherever you choose, such as your phone or Google Drive. It holds your quizzes, scores, sources and interview preps. It doesn't hold your keys.
  • Android's own backup may copy ReThinky's data to your Google account, as it does for most apps. Your keys can't be restored from it, since the key that unlocks them never leaves your phone, so you add them again after a restore.

Reminders

Reminders to review are scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere to make them work.

Payment

If you buy ReThinky through Google Play, Google handles the payment. 24bit7 never sees your card details.

Children

ReThinky isn't designed for children under 13.

Your Choices

  • Delete any quiz, Stash item or interview prep in the app, and it's gone from your phone.
  • Remove a key in Settings at any time.
  • Uninstalling ReThinky deletes everything it stored on your phone.
  • To ask an AI service to delete what it holds, use that company's own process, linked above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
