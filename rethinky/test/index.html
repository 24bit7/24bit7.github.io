Testing ReThinky

Thank you for helping. This page has everything you need for the fourteen days.

ReThinky turns anything you want to learn into a quiz, called a ReThinky. You give it a web page, a YouTube video, a document or a topic, and it writes the questions. Then it brings questions back just before you'd forget them.

Getting Started (About Five Minutes)

  1. Open the opt-in link and tap Become a Tester.
  2. Install ReThinky from Google Play. It's a paid app, but as a tester you'll see "Test card, always approves" at checkout. Choose it and you won't be charged anything.
  3. Open it and follow the set-up pages.
  4. On Set Up Your AI, tap Get Your Key under Basic. Sign in to Google, create a free Gemini key and copy it. No payment details are needed.
  5. Back in ReThinky, tap Add Key. Your key should already be in the box. Tap Add.
  6. Under Advanced, pick Claude, tap Add Key, paste the Claude key you were sent, and tap Add. Leave Performance on Balanced.

Please don't share the Claude key. It's only for this test and stops working when the test ends.

Day by Day

A few minutes a day is plenty. Every day, start by opening ReThinkies and playing anything on the To Do tab. That's how the spacing gets tested, so it matters more than anything new.

Day one. Set up, then make a ReThinky from a web page: in your browser, share an article to ReThinky. Play it.
Day two. Make one from a topic you're curious about, using Topic on the New ReThinky page.
Day three. Make one from a YouTube video: share it from the YouTube app to ReThinky. Tap Watch under an answer to jump to that moment.
Day four. Make one from a document on your phone, such as a PDF or Word file. Choose the Commitment kind this time.
Day five. During a round, tap the cog on a question you think could be better, pick what's wrong and send it for review. Later, open Review and pick a rewrite.
Day six. Try Interview Prep. Find a real job advert on any job site (a customer service or team leader role suits the CV below) and use Jordan's made-up CV, not your own.
Day seven. Explore Settings: the accent colour, text size and What to Call Them.
Day eight. In your browser, open a page, tap Print and choose Save to ReThinky. ReThinky shows you how to switch it on the first time.
Day nine. Make a Best Before ReThinky with a date about a week away, as if you had an exam.
Day ten. Open one of your ReThinkies, go to Quiz Settings and try Add More Questions, then Check for Accuracy.
Day eleven. Share a few things to ReThinky from other apps, then look in the Stash.
Day twelve. Read some Fun Facts.
Day thirteen. Archive a ReThinky, then find it in the Archive and restore it.
Day fourteen. Play your To Do one last time, and tell us the one thing you'd change.

Files to Use

  • Jordan Pryce's made-up CV, for Interview Prep: PDF or Word. Jordan isn't real, so it's safe to send to an AI.
  • Stuck for something to learn? Try the Wikipedia page on honey bees, or any video from the TED-Ed YouTube channel.
